Welcome to OmegaSim Tipps

This wiki collects knowledge and help around OmegaSim, a free app for controlling Carrera HYBRID cars via Bluetooth: with its own driving feel, races, NPC opponents, safety car, pit stops and multiplayer. There are currently 66 articles here. The wiki is written in German; translations are being added step by step.

▶ Play now in the browser
Chrome or Edge with Web Bluetooth, on a PC or Android phone
⬇ Android app (APK)
needed for Wi-Fi multiplayer and the fastest radio link
⌨ Source code on GitHub (Luuke42/omegasim)
MIT licence, report bugs via “Issues”

Getting started

  1. Open OmegaSim in the browser or install the Android app.
  2. Switch on the car and pair it in the Garage with + Connect a car.
  3. Under Options → Driving feel choose a preset; Arcade is best to start with.
  4. Drive off in the Cockpit or start a race type under Race.
  5. If the car hangs: Car does not respond.
